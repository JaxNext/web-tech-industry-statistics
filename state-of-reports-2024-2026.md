## Notes

- **Three different "State of AI" reports** are easy to confuse:
  - Air Street Capital — [State of AI Report](https://www.stateof.ai/) (research/industry/policy analysis, PDF + slides)
  - Devographics — [State of AI](https://2026.stateofai.dev/) (developer survey about AI coding tools and spend)
  - Vercel — [State of AI](https://vercel.com/state-of-ai) (what people build with AI; one-off survey wave, not a yearly series)
- **Devographics** also runs a hub page per survey listing every past edition: [stateofjs.com](https://stateofjs.com/), [stateofreact.com](https://stateofreact.com/), [stateofcss.com](https://stateofcss.com/), [stateofhtml.com](https://stateofhtml.com/), [stateofdevs.com](https://stateofdevs.com/).
- **State of GraphQL** stopped in 2022, so it has no 2024–2026 edition.
- **DORA** renamed its annual report from "Accelerate State of DevOps" to "State of AI-assisted Software Development" in 2025.
- Some pages are rolling/undated (e.g. Monterail's State of Vue.js, Vercel's State of AI) and may be overwritten by a newer edition later.
